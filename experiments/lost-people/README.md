# Getting the lost people back: free analyses

Ember's check removes the detector's false alarms. It finds nobody the detector missed, and it dismisses some real people. The map [Get the lost people back](https://github.com/napiermd/open-water-search/issues/29) looks for a system that recovers them. These folders hold its free analyses. None made an Ember call.

| Folder | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| [dismissed-people](dismissed-people/) | Why does Ember's check dismiss real people? | Of 210 dismissed, 81 were people Ember saw but judged off centre, 89 were centred people it took for an animal, foam or an unclear object, 23 were on a board, boat or float, and 15 were on a beach. |
| [flight-ranking](flight-ranking/) | Which MOBDrone flights are hard for the detector? | The live clip's flight ranks 64th of 66. Thirteen flights of 45 seconds or more are shortlisted for a harder test. |
