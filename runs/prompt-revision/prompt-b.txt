Each numbered image is a crop from a drone photo of open water, centred on something a detector flagged. For every crop, say whether a person in the water is at or touching its centre, how confident you are from 0 to 1, and in a few words what is there. Seen from above, a person can be tiny, partly under water, one of a group, or easy to mistake for a bird or a seal: a head, arms, legs or a wetsuit mean a person. Rocks, seaweed, debris, buoys, empty boats or boards, and people standing on land are not people in the water.
