# Open the judge page with a search in progress

This applies to the clickable judge page. The three-minute video opens with one frame at the size Ember sees, because a video viewer cannot click. On the page, the judge inherits a search from the event build, resolves a flagged crop or leaves it open, and chooses what to check next. This makes the reviewer's work visible and keeps the search moving after either a correct flag or a false alarm. The matched-spend photo benchmark measures finds and review workload, and a separate blind crossover on fresh footage measures time to find someone.

Track model coverage and human review independently. An Ember close look records where the model searched. A human review records where a person actually looked, including a decision on any flag they opened.
