# Land on how it works, one click from the search

The site's front page now explains the tool before asking anything of the visitor. It shows the three steps on one real validation frame, the same two close looks spent by Ember and by the fixed grid, the one-zoom results on the 30 validation frames, and the officer's account. The judge page still opens with a search in progress, as ADR 0003 describes, and the landing page's first button leads straight to it.

A judge who arrives from the submission link may not know what a tile, a close look or a coverage map is. The landing page teaches those terms with a picture before the judge has to use them.
